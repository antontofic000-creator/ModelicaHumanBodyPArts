# Tofic Anton — creator of Lynkorr

**Tofic Anton** is the creator of **Lynkorr**, a movement research and teaching approach developed through Argentine tango, and the author of *The Lynkorr Project*. His official website is **[lynkorr.com](https://www.lynkorr.com/)**.

He also publishes engineering research as **Toufic Antoun El Halabi**, also known as **Toufic El Halabi**. These names refer to the same person. His public researcher identifier is [ORCID 0009-0000-5409-0417](https://orcid.org/0009-0000-5409-0417).

Lynkorr teaching explores standing-leg support, the relationship with the ground, continuous weight transfer, partner connection and spirals in walking and turning. The official website presents the approach in the author's own words, with class demonstrations and written explanations.
